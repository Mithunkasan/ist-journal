📝
Manuscript Submission
Author
The Author prepares and submits the research manuscript through the journal's online submission system. The submission package typically includes the main manuscript, abstract, cover letter, figures, supplementary files, author declarations, and conflict-of-interest statements.
01
🔍
Initial Screening
Editor
The Editor-in-Chief performs an initial screening to verify the manuscript's alignment with the journal's scope, quality standards, formatting requirements, and ethical guidelines. This stage checks for plagiarism, data integrity, authorship, and basic scientific soundness before investing reviewer resources.
02
?
Passes Initial Screening?
No Desk Rejection
The manuscript is rejected without peer review. The Author is notified with reasons. The submission process terminates here.
Yes Proceeds to Review
The manuscript meets the journal's standards and is forwarded to an Associate Editor for further handling.
🧩
Associate Editor Assignment & Preliminary Assessment
Associate Editor
The Editor assigns the manuscript to an Associate Editor whose expertise aligns with the manuscript's subject area. The Associate Editor conducts a preliminary assessment and identifies suitable reviewers based on their domain knowledge, publication record, and availability. Conflicts of interest are carefully checked.
03
🙈 Single-Blind Review
Reviewers know the authors' identities, but authors do not know who reviewed their work.
🙈🙈 Double-Blind Review
Both reviewer and author identities are concealed from each other during the review process.
🙈🙈🙈 Triple-Blind Review
Reviewers, authors, and editors are all anonymous, maximizing objectivity.
🌐 Open Peer Review
Identities of reviewers and authors are known to all parties; reviews may be published.
🔬
Peer Review Evaluation
Reviewer
Selected Reviewers independently evaluate the manuscript and submit detailed review reports covering scientific quality, originality, methodology, statistical validity, ethical considerations, clarity of presentation, and overall significance. Each reviewer provides a recommendation to accept, reject, or request revisions.
04
📋
Associate Editor Recommendation
Associate Editor
The Associate Editor synthesizes all reviewer feedback and weighs the individual recommendations to form a consolidated editorial recommendation. This recommendation, along with the collated reviewer reports, is submitted to the Editor for the final editorial decision.
05
⚖
Editorial Decision
❌ Reject
The manuscript does not meet the journal's standards and is rejected. The Author is notified. The workflow concludes.
✅ Accept
The manuscript is accepted as-is. Proceeds directly to the production and publication stage.
📝 Minor Revision
Small corrections required. Author revises and resubmits; typically re-evaluated by Associate Editor only.
🔄 Major Revision
Substantial rework needed. Manuscript returns for a full review cycle with reviewers after revision.
✏️
Author Revision & Resubmission
Author
The Author revises the manuscript in response to the editorial decision and reviewer comments. A point-by-point response letter is prepared alongside the revised manuscript, clearly addressing each comment. The revised package is resubmitted through the system, triggering another review cycle if required.
06
🔄
Review Cycle(s) — may repeat until all concerns are addressed
✅
Final Recommendation to Editor
Associate Editor
After all revision cycles are complete and reviewer concerns have been satisfactorily addressed, the Associate Editor provides a final consolidated recommendation to the Editor, indicating that the manuscript is ready for a publication decision.
07
⚖
Final Publication Decision
❌ Rejected
If concerns remain unresolved or the manuscript does not meet the bar, it is rejected. The workflow concludes here.
✅ Accepted
Manuscript is formally accepted for publication. It proceeds to the production and typesetting stage.
⚙️
Production & Publication Preparation
Production
The accepted manuscript enters the production pipeline handled by the journal's production team. All activities are carried out to prepare the final article for publication.
📖 Copyediting 🎨 Formatting 🔎 Proofreading 🖨️ Typesetting 🔗 DOI Assignment
08
🎉

Article Published

The research article is officially published in the journal and made freely available to the global readership.
